ABOUT DRIFTLINE

A community group for Sydney's coast

Driftline organises local days for people who want to care for beaches, rockpools and coastal paths. Volunteers collect litter, record intertidal species and help restore native plants.

You can join for a single morning. The event details explain the work, meeting point and what to bring.
